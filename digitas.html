Digitas Internship Presentation

I worked in a team of eight interns to design and present a new marketing campaign for Sephora that was rooted in music and targeted BIPOC Gen-Z.

As a data analyst intern, I was helped to develop the measurement section of the presentation. This section focused on evaluating campaign performance across various channels, such as in-person concerts, digital engagement, and social media, with specific key performance indicators (KPIs) like brand lift, conversion rates, and social media mentions.

The budget section provided an allocation for different campaign channels, demonstrating how each component contributes to Sephora's overall strategy within the total budget of $25 million. The spending is structured across in-person concerts, out-of-home (OOH) advertising, digital platforms, social media, influencer partnerships, and a Spotify collaboration. Each allocation is designed to maximize brand exposure and engagement with the target BIPOC Gen Z demographic.
